Techken, modern looks

Six directions that drop pixel art entirely. Each frame is the same moment: the Software Engineer swings a keyboard at the Database Admin, who guards behind a database stack, with a QA bug on the floor and a coffee pickup on the shelf. The characters here are quick code-built stand-ins, so judge the look and mood, not the finished detail.

3D, fights stay side-on (2.5D)
2D vector, skeletal animation

The gameplay doesn't change with any of these. The simulation, netcode and hitboxes stay exactly as they are. Only the renderer and the art pipeline are replaced.

Animation also gets smoother. 3D and vector rigs animate with bones, so a new move is a new animation clip instead of hand-drawing every frame.
