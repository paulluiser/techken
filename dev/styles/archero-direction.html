Techken: mobile 3D direction

Art direction taken from Archero 2's production style and applied to Techken's own world: super-deformed chibi office workers, soft glossy 3D, chunky block arenas, loud VFX and gold-trimmed UI. Nothing is copied from that game: characters, icons, layouts and the tech-office theme are ours. These are code-built stand-ins, so judge the direction, not model quality.

2-head chibi

Huge heads, stubby limbs, big glossy eyes with two highlights.

Soft 3D, no outlines

Warm key light, rim light in each job's colour, gentle gloss.

Chunky diorama arenas

Beveled carpet tiles and cubicle blocks, camera raised about 25°.
